BloomCycle Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026

Who we are: BloomCycle is provided by Hashir Nafees (the developer, "we"), based in the United Kingdom. For the personal information described in this policy, we are the controller under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018). Contact: peterjhonson56@gmail.com.

Summary

  • BloomCycle has no account, no cloud sync, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Your records are stored only in the app on your device. The app doesn't send them to us (the developer) or anywhere else.
  • Your information leaves the app only when you choose to export or share it, as part of device backups you make, or if you choose to send it to us (for example in a support message).
  • You can delete your records in the app at any time.

What BloomCycle stores, and where

Everything you enter is stored in the app's local database on your device (iPhone, iPad or Mac). Depending on the features you use, this can include:

  • Cycle tracking: period dates and journal entries (flow, symptoms, mood, energy and optional notes).
  • Fertility observations: basal body temperature, LH and pregnancy test results, cervical mucus, notes, and optional private intercourse entries.
  • Pregnancy: pregnancy records and dates you enter, symptoms, appointments and questions, medications, weight, checklists, contraction timings and fetal movement sessions.
  • Postpartum recovery: recovery records, journal entries and follow-up appointments.
  • Baby tracking: baby profiles you create, and their feeding, sleep, diaper and growth records.
  • Settings and preferences: for example your typical cycle length, whether estimates are shown, App Lock, the app's accent color and Today layout.

This is sensitive health information. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not used for advertising, profiling or any purpose other than showing it back to you in the app.

Estimates (for example of your next period, fertile window or due date) are calculated on your device from your own records. They are estimates, not medical advice.

Information the app doesn't collect

The app doesn't send us your health records, and it doesn't collect your name, contact details, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or usage or analytics data. BloomCycle contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code and makes no network requests of its own.

The app connects to Apple only through Apple's own services: the App Store, for subscriptions (see Purchases). Links in the app to this policy, to Apple's Terms of Use and to Apple's subscription management open Apple's screens or your web browser only when you tap them; those pages are covered by the terms and privacy policies of whoever runs them.

If you contact us

If you contact us (for example for support), we receive what you choose to send, such as your name, email address and anything you write or attach. We use it only to reply to you and to help with your question or problem; we don't use it for marketing or anything else. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interests in responding to your messages and supporting BloomCycle; you can object (see Your rights).

Messages sent by email are received in a Gmail account provided by Google, which only the account owner (the developer) can access. Google stores and processes these messages under Google's terms and privacy policy, and may do so on servers outside the United Kingdom, including in the United States. Google states that it is certified under the UK Extension to the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework for transfers from the UK to the United States (see Google's legal frameworks for data transfers). We keep support messages for 12 months after our last interaction with you, then delete them, unless a longer period is required by law.

Please don't include health information, health records or screenshots of them in support messages. If a message does include health information, we won't use it unless you confirm in writing that you agree to us using it to answer your question (your explicit consent); otherwise we'll reply without it and delete it. You can withdraw your consent at any time by asking us to delete your messages.

App Lock

If you turn on App Lock, BloomCycle asks your device to confirm that it's you, using Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode or Mac password. This is handled entirely by Apple's operating system: BloomCycle never receives or stores your biometric data, passcode or password.

App Lock controls access to BloomCycle's screens on your device. It doesn't encrypt your data. How your data is protected when stored depends on your device's security features and settings, for example whether your iPhone or iPad has a passcode (which turns on Apple's Data Protection) and whether FileVault is turned on for your Mac.

Exports, reports and sharing

  • Export Data creates a file named "BloomCycle Export [date].json" containing your records as readable, unencrypted text, which you save or share through your device's standard save or share options. While the save window is open, your device may keep a temporary copy of that file inside BloomCycle; BloomCycle removes it when the export finishes or is cancelled, when you use Delete All Data, and the next time the app starts.
  • Personal reports (a Premium feature) create a PDF containing the sections you choose. Notes and private entries are included only if you choose them. The PDF is kept in a temporary folder on your device, excluded from backups, and removed when you change your choices, close the report screen, use Delete All Data or restart the app.

These files leave BloomCycle only when you choose where to send them (for example to another app, a contact or a storage service). Once shared, a copy is outside BloomCycle's control and is handled by whoever or whatever receives it, under their own terms. Please share health information only with people and services you trust.

Backups

Like other app data on your device, your BloomCycle records may be included in device backups that you make, such as iCloud Backup, Finder backups, or Time Machine on Mac. Those backups are controlled by you and, for iCloud Backup, by Apple under Apple's terms and privacy policy. BloomCycle doesn't sync your data to iCloud or any other service.

Purchases (BloomCycle Premium)

BloomCycle Premium is an optional auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we don't receive your payment details or your Apple Account details through the app. On your device, the app receives information from Apple about your subscription (such as which plan is active and when it renews or ends) only to turn Premium features on or off. Purchases are subject to Apple's terms and Apple's privacy policy. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings or from BloomCycle's Premium screen, which opens Apple's subscription settings.

Crash reports and diagnostics

BloomCycle doesn't include its own crash reporting, analytics or diagnostics service. If the app hits an error, it may write a short technical message to your device's system log, with personal details hidden; this log stays on your device unless you choose to share diagnostics with Apple.

Apple collects crash reports and diagnostic information only if you allow it in your device's Analytics & Improvements settings (under Privacy & Security). If you also turn on Share With App Developers, Apple may give us crash reports and related diagnostic information about BloomCycle. If you test a pre-release version through Apple's TestFlight, crash reports and any feedback you choose to send are shared with us through TestFlight. Apple creates and controls this information under Apple's privacy policy, and you can turn this sharing off at any time in your device settings.

These reports describe the technical state of the app when a problem occurred (for example the code that was running, and device and system versions). BloomCycle doesn't add your records to them, but a report may contain fragments of information that was in use by the app at the time. We use these reports only to troubleshoot and fix problems in BloomCycle, and only the developer can access them.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your records stay on your device until you delete them. You can delete individual records in the app.
  • Delete All Data (in Settings) permanently deletes, from BloomCycle's database on this device: all of your records (cycle, fertility, pregnancy, postpartum and baby tracking); your settings stored with them (for example typical cycle length and whether estimates are shown); and any open report PDFs and temporary export copies.
    It doesn't change these, which are kept separately: App Lock (turn it off in Settings if you want to); the accent color and Today layout you chose, and an internal reference to the baby profile you last viewed (not its name or records); your subscription, which Apple manages; and device backups, and exports or reports you saved or shared earlier.
  • Deleting the app: on iPhone and iPad, deleting BloomCycle removes its data from the device. On a Mac, moving the app to the Trash doesn't remove its data, which stays in the app's container in your user Library; use Delete All Data first if you want it removed.
  • Neither Delete All Data nor deleting the app removes your data from separately stored device backups or from exports and reports you saved or shared. Those are managed wherever you keep them.
  • Because the app never sends us your records, we can't access, correct, export or delete them for you; they're under your control in the app.

Children

BloomCycle is intended for adults aged 18 and over managing their own health information. Baby tracking records are created and kept by the parent or caregiver on their own device and are never sent to us.

Your rights

Because BloomCycle doesn't send your records to us, they're under your control on your device: you can view, export and delete them in the app at any time. We don't hold them, so we can't access, correct or delete them for you.

For personal information we do hold (for example support messages), UK data protection law gives you rights to ask for access to it, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict how we use it, and to object to our using it on the basis of legitimate interests. To use these rights, or for questions about this policy, contact peterjhonson56@gmail.com. We don't make automated decisions about you.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your information, please contact us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK data protection regulator: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Changes to this policy

If the way BloomCycle handles information changes, we'll update this policy and its effective date before the change takes effect, and describe significant changes in the app or its App Store release notes.
