BloomCycle is not medical advice

BloomCycle is a private journal. Its period, fertile-window and due-date estimates are estimates, not medical advice, and it must not be used as contraception. For health questions, talk to a qualified health professional. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services.

Your data

Where is my data stored?

Only in BloomCycle on your device. There's no account and no cloud sync, and the app doesn't send your records to us. Your data may be included in device backups you make, such as iCloud Backup, Finder backups or Time Machine.

Can I move my data to a new device?

Restoring a device backup to the new device brings BloomCycle's data with it. BloomCycle doesn't sync between your devices.

How do I export my data?

In Settings, choose Export Data…. BloomCycle creates a readable, unencrypted JSON file of your records that you save or share where you choose. Keep it somewhere safe.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual records where you made them, or use Delete All Data… in Settings. Delete All Data doesn't affect device backups, or exports and reports you saved or shared earlier. On a Mac, moving the app to the Trash doesn't remove its data; use Delete All Data first.

App Lock

Turn on App Lock in Settings to require Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode or Mac password when BloomCycle opens. It hides the app's screens; it doesn't encrypt your data, which is protected by your device's own security.

BloomCycle Premium

What does Premium include?

Optional customization. Tracking stays free.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Open BloomCycle's Premium screen and choose to manage your subscription, or go to your Apple Account's subscription settings. If you cancel, Premium stays active until the end of the period you've paid for.

I bought Premium but it isn't active

Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe, then use Restore Purchases on the Premium screen.

Refunds

Purchases are made through Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
